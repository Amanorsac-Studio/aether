AETHER is installed

Rescan your plug-ins and AETHER will appear under Amanorsac Studio.

What next: open it and enter your licence key. Your key is in My Apps on amanorsac.studio, and in your purchase email. Until it is activated AETHER stays silent.

To remove it later, run Uninstall AETHER.command in /Library/Application Support/Amanorsac Studio/AETHER/. Your presets are kept.
