AETHER 1.0.0

Dynamic Air Exciter · Amanorsac Studio

AETHER adds air to a recording: tunable presence and air bands, a harmonic exciter that generates top end that was never there, and a sibilance-aware limiter that keeps the sparkle and drops the harshness.
