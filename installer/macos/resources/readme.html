What gets installed

  • VST3 — /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/AETHER.vst3
  • Audio Unit — /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/AETHER.component
  • Uninstaller and this Read Me — /Library/Application Support/Amanorsac Studio/AETHER/

Your own files — presets you save stay in ~/Documents/Amanorsac Studio/AETHER/ and are never touched by installing, updating or uninstalling.

After installing — rescan plug-ins in your DAW. Logic and GarageBand pick up the Audio Unit; Studio One, Live, Reaper, Cubase and Pro Tools use the VST3.

Activating — open AETHER and enter your key from My Apps on amanorsac.studio. It works offline for 30 days at a stretch once activated.

Uninstalling — run /Library/Application Support/Amanorsac Studio/AETHER/Uninstall AETHER.command. It removes the plug-ins and keeps your presets.

Requirements — macOS 10.13 or later, Intel or Apple Silicon.
